---
question: 'Should I switch to the latest version of OurDNA?'
---

With 730,947 exomes and 76,215 genomes, the OurDNA v4.1 call set is the largest of all the OurDNA versions. It also contains nearly all data from prior versions (v2 and v3) except a small number of samples excluded due to data quality and updated sample filtering pipelines. Since OurDNA v4.1 is mapped to GRCh38, if you haven’t switched to GRCh38, now is the time!
