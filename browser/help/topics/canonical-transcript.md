id: canonical-transcript
title: Ensembl canonical transcript
---

The canonical transcript is [MANE](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/refseq/MANE/) Select, when available, as the default/primary transcript for reporting annotations.

Where MANE Select is not available, the canonical transcript is generally the longest transcript, though not necessarily the most biologically relevant. It is defined in the [Ensembl glossary](https://ensembl.org/Help/Glossary).
